A handwritten derivation of the large-sample properties of a Generalized Linear Model (GLM) beta estimator. By applying a first-order Taylor approximation to the score function around the true parameter \(\beta \), this proof demonstrates how the estimator achieves asymptotic unbiasedness and consistency as \(n \rightarrow \infty\).
